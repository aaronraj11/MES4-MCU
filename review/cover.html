MES4-MCU · design review package R3

Input for schematic review R3 · schematic Rev A-d6 (title block 14 Oct 2026; notes updated 26 and 29 Oct) · 29 Oct 2026 · Monkey Robotics · Aaron Raj Bhatti

What's in this package: (1) this cover: what changed since review R2, the status of every R2 finding and what to look at; (2) the LTspice simulation report, 10 benches plus a model check, grouped by schematic sheet; (3) the 5-page schematic (A3), appended after the report. The carrier interface specification (MES4-CIF) is referenced but not included. Its must-follow rules are printed on schematic sheet 4.

The module: STM32G0B1KBT6 on an M.2 2242 B-key card (custom pinout, not a PC M.2 card), 5 V in, TLV76733 3.3 V LDO, SN65HVD230 CAN at 500 kbit/s, 4 status LEDs, passive buzzer and a 5 V monitor. Rev A is not fabricated yet.

C · What to review

  • The schematic as a whole (the five A3 pages at the end), especially what changed in d5 / d6: the passive buzzer drive on Power and the LQFP-32 U1 on MCU.
  • Buzzer current (report §3, BZ1). The bench gives 69–95 mA extra and 0.3–0.5 W in the coil while beeping, against the datasheet's 80 mA. Is the PWM drive with the D3 freewheel acceptable as is, or should the default duty be lower?
  • Negative +5V transients (report §3, PWR3). The module relies on the carrier (rule 4) to keep +5V above −0.3 V. Is that acceptable, or should the module get its own protection?
  • Model assumptions (report §7). U2 and U3 are behavioural models, and X1's edge rate and BZ1's inductance are assumed, not published. Check that the conclusions don't hang on them.
